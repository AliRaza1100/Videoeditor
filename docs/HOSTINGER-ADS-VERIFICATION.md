Ads stay outside active encoding. Export normally stays available. Reward callbacks grant thirty ad-free minutes in the current session; cancellation, timeout or no-fill does not earn a reward and can continue ordinary exporting. Demo units do not prove third-party network serving or earnings.

The source compiles; these statements do not claim a live ad impression or earnings. The existing 1.15 APK predates this update.

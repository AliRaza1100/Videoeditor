## App name

Lumen Studio

## Short description

Edit videos and photos with text, music, filters, effects and local drafts.

## Full description

Turn your clips and photos into a story with Lumen Studio. Arrange your media on a timeline, add your own words and music, and save an editable draft to continue later.

EDIT YOUR WAY
• Trim, split and arrange clips.
• Crop, rotate and adjust your frame.
• Change clip speed and shape speed curves.
• Adjust color and explore filters and effects.

ADD YOUR PERSONAL TOUCH
• Add text with font, style, color and animation controls.
• Place stickers and image overlays.
• Adjust supported layer timing on the timeline.
• Import music and audio from your device.

KEEP CREATING
• Save editable drafts on your device.
• Reopen projects from your recent work or project library.
• Choose a theme that suits your workspace.

EXPORT AND SHARE
Choose an available export resolution and save your video as an MP4. Share the finished file using Android's sharing options.

YOUR MEDIA, ON YOUR DEVICE
Lumen Studio processes your selected media locally. This edition has no account, advertisements or analytics, and does not upload your editing projects to a developer server. Projects stay on the device where they are created; cloud sync is not included.

Only the media you choose is accessed through Android's picker or Files. Files selected from a cloud provider may require that provider to download them. External sharing and email services follow their own privacy practices.

Format support, preview performance and export options depend on your device and source media. A higher export resolution cannot restore detail missing from the original. Keep important source files and check your exported video before deleting originals.

# Privacy Policy for Lumen Studio

Effective date: [PUBLICATION DATE]

Lumen Studio is provided by [DEVELOPER OR COMPANY NAME], referred to as "we" in this policy. This policy explains how the local edition of Lumen Studio handles information when you edit media, save projects, export files or contact us.

Privacy and support contact: [MONITORED SUPPORT EMAIL]

### 1. Media you choose

Lumen Studio accesses photos, videos and audio you select through Android's media picker or Files. It does not request permission to access your entire photo or video library. We use selected media to provide editing, previews, drafts and exports. Processing within the app takes place on your device.

Android may offer media from a cloud file provider. Downloading that media is handled by Android or that provider under its own privacy practices.

### 2. Information stored on your device

Drafts contain references to media, filenames, clip timing, editing settings and text you enter. The app may also store private media copies when needed to retain access, imported or extracted sounds, generated stickers, freeze frames and temporary preview or export files.

The app saves preferences such as theme, language, notification choices and the version of the terms you accepted. These records are kept locally. Projects are specific to this installation and are not automatically synced to another device.

### 3. Collection and sharing

This edition has no user accounts, advertising, analytics or remote notification service. Lumen Studio does not upload your media, drafts, authored text or audio to a developer server, and we do not sell this information.

Exports are written to the destination you choose. Sharing opens Android's sharing flow. The recipient app, service or file provider you choose then handles the file under its own terms and privacy practices.

### 4. Contacting support

Contact Us lets you enter a reason and description. Send opens your email app with those details; the message is sent only if you send it from that email app. Lumen Studio does not automatically attach media or diagnostic files.

If you email us, we receive the message, your email address and any other information you include. We use it to respond to your enquiry and resolve support issues. Your email provider and our email provider process the correspondence to deliver and store it. Avoid sending passwords or sensitive media.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to resolve your enquiry, maintain necessary support records or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion; where retention is required by law, we will explain the applicable limitation.

### 5. Permissions and reminders

Media access follows your selections in Android's picker. The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or device-wide storage permissions.

Notifications are optional and off by default. When enabled, generic local editing reminders are scheduled approximately every three hours, including overnight, subject to Android delivery rules. You can disable them in the app's Settings or Android's notification settings.

### 6. Retention and deletion

Drafts remain until you delete them or remove app data. Creating a new draft does not automatically delete an older draft. Delete individual drafts in Projects and imported sounds in the sound library. Deleting a draft does not remove original media or every separate app-generated file.

To remove all app-private editing data and preferences, use Android Settings > Apps > Lumen Studio > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Labels vary by device. Android may also clear temporary caches.

Originals and exports stored outside app-private storage remain under your control. Delete them using Android Files or the relevant provider. Removing source files or revoking access may prevent a saved draft from reopening correctly.

There is no Lumen Studio account to delete. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete projects stored only on your device.

### 7. Security

Private editing files are held in Android's app-private storage, and app backup is disabled. Device security and the services or destinations you choose also affect the protection of your files. No storage or communication method can guarantee absolute security.

### 8. Your choices

You choose which media to import, where to export it and whether to share it or enable notifications. You can manage app permissions in Android settings and remove local data as explained above. Contact us about support correspondence or other privacy enquiries. Any applicable statutory privacy rights remain available to you.

### 9. Policy changes

We will update this policy and its effective date when our practices change. Changes involving cloud processing, advertising, analytics or other new data uses will be reflected in the app's disclosures and store declarations before release, with any consent required by applicable law or platform rules.
